Rabeya Khan

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering researcher working across two complementary strengths: computational materials modeling and hands-on hardware and embedded systems engineering, applied independently to semiconductor devices, robotics, and precision agriculture.

01 About

I am a graduate researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Jamalpur Science & Technology University (JSTU), currently working as a Research Assistant at Robo Tech Valley, Dhaka. My undergraduate research, under the supervision of Dr. Md. Rasidul Islam, centered on computational modeling and efficiency enhancement of lead-free perovskite solar cells, combining first-principles DFT simulation, device-level modeling (SCAPS-1D), and machine learning validation. This work has led to two first-author publications in peer-reviewed journals.

Separately, I have strong practical experience in circuit design, microcontroller programming, and embedded systems building and prototyping physical hardware in the lab, including robotics and IoT systems for real-time environmental monitoring, disease detection in crops and livestock, and an ongoing government-funded self-driving assistive system for visually impaired individuals. Machine learning is the one thread that runs through several of these projects as a shared tool, though materials research, robotics, and agriculture are each pursued as independent research directions.

I care about research that survives contact with the real world: models that hold up under domain shift, simulations validated against data, and hardware built under real constraints rather than idealized ones. My long-term goal is a career that combines advanced research with technical teaching.
