Personal Info

Rabeya Khan

I work at the intersection of computational materials science, machine learning, and embedded systems — modeling lead-free perovskite solar cells at the device level, and building field-robust vision models and IoT systems for agriculture and energy monitoring in Bangladesh.

01 About

I am a graduate researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Jamalpur Science & Technology University (JSTU), currently working as a Research Assistant at Robo Tech Valley, Dhaka. My undergraduate research, under the supervision of Dr. Md. Rasidul Islam, centered on computational modeling and efficiency enhancement of lead-free perovskite solar cells, combining first-principles DFT simulation with device-level modeling and machine learning validation. Alongside this, I build applied machine learning and embedded systems for real-world deployment — disease detection in crops and livestock, and real-time environmental monitoring.

I care about research that survives contact with the real world: models that hold up under domain shift, simulations that are validated against data, and systems built under real hardware constraints rather than idealized ones. My long-term goal is a career that combines advanced research with technical teaching.

02 Research Interests

MATERIALS
Computational Photovoltaics
DFT and device-level simulation (Quantum ESPRESSO, SCAPS-1D) of lead-free and eco-friendly perovskite absorbers for next-generation solar cells.
VISION
Robust Machine Learning
YOLOv8 and Vision Transformer models for agricultural and veterinary disease detection, with a focus on lab-to-field domain robustness and explainability.
SYSTEMS
Embedded & IoT Systems
Real-time sensing and cloud-integrated monitoring platforms (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi) for energy and environmental applications.
